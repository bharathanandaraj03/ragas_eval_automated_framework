# Orbitly Support and Service Level Agreement

> Fictional company and product, created as demo data for RAG evaluation.

## Support channels by plan

| Plan | Channels | Hours | First-response target |
|------|----------|-------|-----------------------|
| Starter | Email, help centre | Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00 US Central | 2 business days |
| Business | Email, live chat | Mon–Fri, 7:00–19:00 US Central | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone, dedicated CSM | 24/7 | 1 hour for Severity 1 |

## Severity levels

- **Severity 1 (Critical)**: Orbitly is down or unusable for all users in a workspace, or data loss is occurring.
- **Severity 2 (High)**: A core module (Boards, Timeline, Insights, or Automations) is unavailable, but a workaround exists.
- **Severity 3 (Normal)**: A non-critical feature is impaired or a question about usage.
- **Severity 4 (Low)**: Feature requests and cosmetic issues.

Only Enterprise customers receive 24/7 handling of Severity 1 incidents. On Starter and Business, Severity 1 tickets are handled during the plan's support hours.

## Uptime SLA

Orbitly commits to the following monthly uptime:

- Starter: no formal SLA (best effort).
- Business: 99.9% uptime.
- Enterprise: 99.95% uptime.

Scheduled maintenance, announced at least 72 hours in advance, does not count as downtime. Maintenance windows are Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, eligible customers receive service credits on their next invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit (% of monthly fee) |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| Below SLA but ≥ 99.0% | 10% |
| ≥ 95.0% and < 99.0% | 25% |
| < 95.0% | 50% |

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Service credits are the customer's sole remedy for SLA breaches and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Status page

Live service status and incident history are published at status.orbitly.example. Customers can subscribe to updates by email or SMS.
