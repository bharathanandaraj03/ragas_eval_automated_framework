# Orbitly Product Overview

> Fictional company and product, created as demo data for RAG evaluation.

Orbitly is a cloud-based project management platform built by Orbitly Labs, headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company was founded in 2019 by Priya Raman and Marcus Hale. Orbitly serves more than 4,200 business customers in 38 countries.

## Core modules

Orbitly is organised into four modules:

1. **Boards** – Kanban and Scrum boards with swimlanes, WIP limits, and custom columns.
2. **Timeline** – Gantt-style planning with task dependencies and critical-path highlighting.
3. **Insights** – Dashboards for velocity, cycle time, and burndown. Insights is only available on the Business and Enterprise plans.
4. **Automations** – No-code rules such as "when a task moves to Done, notify the reporter". The Starter plan allows 50 automation runs per month; Business allows 5,000; Enterprise is unlimited.

## Supported platforms

- Web app: latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.
- Desktop apps: Windows 10 or later, macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.
- Mobile apps: iOS 16 or later, Android 11 or later.
- Linux desktop is not supported; Linux users should use the web app.

## Integrations

Orbitly integrates natively with Slack, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, GitLab, Jira (one-way import only), Google Drive, and Zapier. The Jira integration imports projects, issues, and comments, but changes made in Orbitly are not synced back to Jira.

## Workspaces and limits

A workspace is the top-level container for projects and members. Each workspace can hold up to 500 projects. A single project can contain up to 100,000 tasks. File attachments are limited to 250 MB per file on all plans.

## Terminology

- **Reporter**: the person who created a task.
- **Assignee**: the person responsible for completing a task. A task can have at most one assignee but up to 20 watchers.
- **Sprint**: a fixed time-box, configurable from 1 to 4 weeks.
