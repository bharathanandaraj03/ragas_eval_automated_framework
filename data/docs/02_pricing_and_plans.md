# Orbitly Pricing and Plans

> Fictional company and product, created as demo data for RAG evaluation.

Orbitly offers three paid plans and a free trial. All prices are in US dollars, per user, per month.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Minimum seats | Storage per workspace |
|------|-----------------|----------------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Starter | $9 | $7 | 3 | 100 GB |
| Business | $19 | $15 | 5 | 1 TB |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Custom quote | 50 | Unlimited |

Annual billing saves roughly 20% compared with monthly billing.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required to start a trial. When the trial ends, the workspace is downgraded to read-only mode until a paid plan is selected. Data in read-only workspaces is kept for 60 days, after which it is permanently deleted.

## Discounts

- **Non-profit organisations** receive 50% off the Starter and Business plans after verification.
- **Education** (accredited schools and universities) receive Business at the Starter price.
- Discounts cannot be combined with each other.

## Adding and removing seats

Seats can be added at any time and are charged on a prorated basis for the rest of the billing period. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal date; no partial refunds are issued for removed seats mid-term.

## Enterprise-only features

The Enterprise plan includes SAML single sign-on (SSO), SCIM user provisioning, audit logs retained for 2 years, a dedicated customer success manager, and a 99.95% uptime SLA. See the Support and SLA document for details.

## Payment methods

Starter and Business can be paid by credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms. PayPal and cryptocurrency are not accepted.
