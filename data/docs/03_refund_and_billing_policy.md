# Orbitly Refund and Billing Policy

> Fictional company and product, created as demo data for RAG evaluation.

## Refund eligibility

- **Monthly plans**: Orbitly does not issue refunds for monthly subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the current billing month.
- **Annual plans**: Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of the initial annual purchase. After 30 days, a prorated refund is available for the unused full months remaining, minus a 10% processing fee.
- **Renewals**: Annual renewals are refundable within 14 days of the renewal date. After 14 days, renewals are non-refundable.
- **Enterprise contracts**: Refund terms are defined in the customer's signed order form and override this policy.

## How to request a refund

Only a workspace **Owner** or **Billing Admin** can request a refund. Requests are submitted from Settings → Billing → Request refund, or by emailing billing@orbitly.example. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Orbitly retries the charge 3 times over 7 days. The workspace Owner is emailed after each failed attempt. If all retries fail, the workspace enters a 14-day grace period with full access. After the grace period, the workspace is suspended (read-only) until payment is updated.

## Taxes and invoices

Prices exclude sales tax and VAT. Tax is calculated based on the billing address. Invoices are generated on each billing date and can be downloaded as PDF from Settings → Billing → Invoices. Invoices for the past 7 years are available.

## Currency

All charges are made in US dollars. Orbitly does not currently support billing in other currencies; the customer's bank sets any conversion fees.
